# Using a board

## The boards

The boards to use at a given moment are on the lists of [welland](https://welland.fpgas.online/fpgas/),
[ps1](https://ps1.fpgas.online/fpgas/) and [Tiny Tapeout](https://tinytapeout.fpgas.online/).

- [Acorn](acorn/index.md): an Acorn on welland, and how to load a design onto it.
- [Tiny Tapeout FPGA demo board](tt-fpga/index.md): run a bundled design, or load your own.
- [NeTV2](netv2/index.md): a NeTV2 on welland, and how to load a design onto it.
- [Fomu EVT](fomu-evt/index.md): a Fomu EVT on welland, and why a visitor cannot load a design onto it today.
- [Arty A7](arty-a7/index.md): an Arty A7 on welland and ps1, and how to load a design onto one.
- [Tiny Tapeout chip demo boards](tt-asic/index.md): select a project in the chip on tinytapeout.fpgas.online, and drive it.

## Every board's page

- [The board page on welland and ps1](board-page.md): each part of a board's page, and what it does.
- [How to reset a board](reset.md): start a board and its host again.
- [The camera on a board](camera.md): the two views, and what the camera points at.
- [Boards](../../boards/index.md), under Admin documentation: setting a board up, wiring it and checking it.
