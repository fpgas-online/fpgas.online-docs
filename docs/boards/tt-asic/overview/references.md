# Tiny Tapeout chip demo board references

This page lists the outside documents and the other pages of this site that the Tiny Tapeout chip demo board pages rest on.
It repeats none of their content.

## Outside documents

- [Tiny Tapeout pinout recommendations](https://tinytapeout.com/specs/pinouts/): how a design's pins are laid out.
- [Tiny Tapeout PCB specifications](https://tinytapeout.com/specs/pcb/): the demo board's connectors and signals, for the TT04 to TT08 boards.
- [Tiny Tapeout demo PCB design](https://github.com/TinyTapeout/tt-demo-pcb): the demo board's hardware repository.
- [Tiny Tapeout MicroPython SDK](https://github.com/TinyTapeout/tt-micropython-firmware): the demo board firmware.
- [`fpgas-tt`](https://github.com/fpgas-online/fpgas.online-tt): the Pi-side bridge daemon.
- [The public boards](https://tinytapeout.fpgas.online): the boards a visitor can use.

- [Tiny Tapeout PMOD layouts](../../pmod/tinytapeout.md): with the [RP2040 map (v2, TT06 to TT08)](../../pmod/tinytapeout.md#rp2040-gpio-mapping-demo-board-v2-tt06-tt08) and the [RP2350 map (v3, TT09+)](../../pmod/tinytapeout.md#rp2350-gpio-mapping-demo-board-v3-tt09)
- [Raspberry Pi PMOD HAT](../../pmod/rpi-hat.md): the PMOD HAT adapter.
- [Tiny Tapeout FPGA demo board](../../tt-fpga/index.md): the FPGA sibling.
